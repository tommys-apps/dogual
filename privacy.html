Privacy.

Short version: we don't collect anything about you or your dog.

What we collect

Nothing. Dogual doesn't collect, sell or share personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no adverts and no tracking.

On your iPhone

Your dog's details, photo, journal, care reminders and training progress are stored only on your iPhone, and shared with the Dogual widget on the same phone. Deleting a dog in Settings, or deleting the app, deletes them.

Walk weather

If you turn on walk weather, Dogual asks for your approximate location and sends it to Apple Weather to get the local forecast. It isn't stored or sent anywhere else. You can turn walk weather off in Settings at any time.

Notifications

Training and care reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. No server is involved.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Children

Dogual isn't aimed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and the date above. To get in touch, use the support page.
